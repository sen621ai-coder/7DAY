Adaptation for this cargo drone: removed the presentation floor/environment; converted geometry, coordinate handedness and texture layout; reduced textures to at most 2048 pixels; converted PBR maps for Unity Standard; used alpha cutout for body grille holes and reduced emission strength; scaled and centered the animated collision envelope. Mechanical landing/flight poses derive from the source animation at 0 and 25 seconds, with flight turbine planes leveled for hovering. Source root movement, body rocking, demonstration turns and pupil morph animation are not played. The two original turbine meshes rotate around their measured local axes. No Oakraven assets are included.
